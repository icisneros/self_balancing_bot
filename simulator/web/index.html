A model, with room for reality.

A planar inverted pendulum with motor lag, wheel torque reaction, friction and a delayed IMU. Physical defaults and IMU mounting come from the robot YAML file. Mounting angles affect pitch; IMU position affects the side-view drawing only. No yaw motion, slip, electrical faults, I2C transfer time, serial blocking, or DMP sensor fusion is simulated. Gains that work here need validation on your robot.

The measured trace is sensor pitch relative to its configured upright reading. The graphs show the whole computed trial. Animation and scrubbing replay that trial; changing controls marks it for a new run.
